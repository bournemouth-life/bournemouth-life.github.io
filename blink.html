BINF Link

BINF Link is a project developed by a BU MSc Internet of Things student. It aids nuerodivergent people who do not have full time care provision to connect with volunteer supporters in the local area. It uses Wireless Local Area Network (WLAN) technology as the communication backbone. Volunteers may also be neurodivergent themselves.

The access points also operate as a webserver giving local information and raising awareness of neurodiversity. The webserver has other uses including supporting the persecuted church in China and evangelism activity of non-verbal people.

BINF Link is currently undergoing range testing and field trails. A detailed report will be available soon.
